# CPM Weighting: Consolidated Head-to-Head with Paired-Bootstrap Significance

Throwaway research. No production files changed, no commit. All numbers
regenerated by `research/weighting_consolidated.py` ->
`research/weighting_consolidated.json`. Weighting/selection delegated to
`research/inverse_vol_weighting.py` (CONT / IV4 / INVVOL-3 / INVVOL-2 / EW-2) and
`research/weighting_headtohead.py` (ERC); bootstrap primitives from
`research/paired_bootstrap_pair_vs_continuous.py`.

## Config / window / execution / method (labeled)

- Sleeve: CPM, U=1 / R=1 / C=1 (production universe, vol-adjusted Faber ranker,
  HYG-OR-TIP any-positive canary). Pool = top-half K=4 trend-qualified
  (positive-Faber) candidates. Selection IDENTICAL across all schemes; only the
  two design axes vary: SUB-SELECTION (how many of the top-4 to hold) x WEIGHTING.
- Universe: QQQ, SPHQ, EFA, EEM, VNQ, GLD, TLT, DBC. Safe: SHV / IEF (best_safe).
- Execution: T+1 MOO exact (`mooex`), realistic open-fill. Cost 10 bps/side
  headline (net); gross = 0 bps.
- Covariance / vol lookback: 504 trading days. Sub-selection uses the
  full-covariance lowest-portfolio-variance m-subset (same `min_var_subset` as
  production pair selection); inverse-vol weighting uses sigma_i = sqrt of the
  diagonal over the same 504d window.
- Windows: CLEAN 18y (2008-05-30 .. 2026-05-22, 17.98y); EXT 27y
  (1999-03-10 .., 27.20y; pre-2008 leans on stitched BIL/AGG proxies).
- Metrics: Sharpe, CAGR, Vol, MaxDD, Calmar (cash = SHV).
- Significance: PAIRED stationary block bootstrap, B=2000, block=21 days,
  seed=42, SAME block index applied to both series in each pair. diff = (A - B);
  for MaxDD both values are negative so diff > 0 means A is shallower (better).

| INVVOL-2 | 0.0222 | 1.076 |
| EW-2 | 0.0168 | 1.000 |
| ERC | 0.0078 | 0.105 |

- Continuous min-var has the highest mean drift among the no-flip schemes
  (0.0226), as expected (full off-diagonal covariance is noisy).
- Whole-top-K inverse-vol (IV4) and ERC have the LOWEST mean drift (0.0078) - no
  discrete sub-selection, so weights only drift smoothly with sigma; both also
  have small max moves (IV4 0.236, ERC 0.105).
- Sub-selection adds discrete flip risk: INVVOL-2 / INVVOL-3 (and EW-2) show
  large max L1 moves (1.076 / 0.636 / 1.000) - a single lookback perturbation can
  flip the held set, causing a near-full basket swap. INVVOL-2 is the worst
  (smallest set => most flip-prone) and its mean drift (0.0222) is on par with
  CONT. INVVOL-3 sits in between (mean 0.0169, below CONT) but still carries the
  discrete-flip tail.

## 5. VERDICT

### Is any contrast statistically significant?

No. Across all five contrasts x three metrics x two windows, **zero 95% CIs
exclude zero**. Every weighting difference is within sampling noise. The
strongest directional signal is INVVOL-3 > IV4 (CLEAN P=0.971 Sharpe, 0.905
Calmar), whose Sharpe CI only just touches zero - i.e. sub-selection helping
inverse-vol is the closest thing to a real effect, but still not formally
significant.

### Whole-top-K inverse-vol (IV4) vs sub-selection - honest call

Sub-selection wins. IV4 is the most diversified and most estimation-stable
scheme (lowest mean L1 0.0078, lowest concentration 0.33, effN 3.7), but it pays
a clear Sharpe penalty (worst CLEAN net Sharpe 1.1491; INVVOL-3 beats it
directionally with P=0.971 on CLEAN Sharpe and P=0.806 on EXT Sharpe, plus
higher Calmar in both windows). The diversification of holding all 4 is not worth
the return drag. Sub-selecting to 3 lowest-variance names recovers the Sharpe and
lifts Calmar at only a modest concentration increase (0.33 -> 0.40) and modest
extra drift (0.0078 -> 0.0169, still below CONT 0.0226).

### Ranking for a DD-aware production objective

Objective weights: Calmar/MaxDD first, Sharpe (within-noise, so a tiebreaker),
paired-significance (none, so favor robustness), estimation-robustness,
concentration cap, simplicity (no solver preferred).

1. **INVVOL-3** - best all-rounder. Top-3 Calmar in both windows (best EXT
   0.9148, 2nd CLEAN 1.0824), shallowest EXT MaxDD (-15.18%), within-noise Sharpe
   vs CONT (loses CLEAN by ~0.05, wins EXT by ~0.016 - both inside CI), beats EW-2
   and IV4 directionally, well-capped concentration (~0.40, no 100% corner),
   estimation drift (0.0169) below CONT, no solver.
2. **INVVOL-2** - statistically tied with INVVOL-3 (2-vs-3 is a coin flip), best
   CLEAN Calmar (1.0945), but more concentrated (~0.55), most estimation-
   sensitive of the no-solver family (mean 0.0222 ~ CONT, worst max-flip L1
   1.076).
3. **CONT** - best CLEAN Sharpe (1.2935) but worst Calmar of the inverse-vol/ERC
   set, most concentrated (0.62, hits 100% corners), most estimation-hungry
   (full cov), needs a solver. Its only edge (CLEAN Sharpe) is within noise and
   does not survive into EXT.
4. **IV4** - most robust and diversified but Sharpe-penalized; better as a
   robustness ceiling than a production pick.
5. **ERC** - mirrors IV4 (diversified, low drift, good MaxDD) but needs a solver
   and posts lower Sharpe; no advantage over IV4 that justifies the solver.
6. **EW-2** (incumbent) - worst Calmar/MaxDD on the grid (-16.35% / -16.76%),
   Sharpe within noise of the leaders. Dominated on the DD-aware objective by
   INVVOL-2/INVVOL-3, which share its selection and only change the weighting.

### Recommended production weighting

**INVVOL-3** (sub-select the 3 lowest-variance of the top-K=4 trend-qualified
names, inverse-vol weight). Rationale: best drawdown-adjusted profile across both
windows among solver-free schemes, Sharpe statistically indistinguishable from
the CONT optimizer, materially lower concentration than CONT (no 100% corner) and
lower estimation drift than CONT, no solver. It directionally beats whole-top-K
IV4 (the one near-significant effect in the study) and dominates the EW-2
incumbent on Calmar/MaxDD while sharing its selection logic. INVVOL-2 is an
acceptable close second (statistically tied) but is more concentrated and more
estimation/flip-sensitive, so INVVOL-3 is the safer DD-aware choice.

## Caveats / confidence

- Single dataset, single execution model (mooex T+1 MOO exact, 10 bps/side),
  in-sample over each full window, no walk-forward re-selection of the scheme.
  EXT pre-2008 uses stitched BIL/AGG proxies.
- No contrast is significant at 95%; the ranking is driven by point estimates +
  directional bootstrap probabilities + robustness/concentration arguments, not
  by proven outperformance. Treat the INVVOL-3 recommendation as the best-balanced
  choice under a DD-aware prior, not a statistically dominant one.
- Sub-selection (INVVOL-2/-3, EW-2) carries discrete-flip tail risk under
  estimation perturbation (large max L1 moves) that the whole-top-K schemes
  (IV4/ERC) avoid; this is the main robustness cost of sub-selection.
- max single weight = 1.000 for CONT is a genuine risk-on solver corner;
  the all-safe defensive-month 100%-in-one-safe-asset artifact is excluded
  (risk-on months only).
- Confidence: HIGH on anchor reproduction (all exact) and on the relative
  ordering / "all within noise" conclusion. MEDIUM on whether INVVOL-3's edge
  over CONT/IV4 survives out-of-sample / walk-forward.
