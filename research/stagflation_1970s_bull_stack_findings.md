# Stagflation 1970s: ACTUAL combined BULL stack vs HAA-Simple

**1970s BACKTEST ONLY. No production or memo implication.** This runs the full
production BULL stack as ONE strategy (canary AND trend AND vol), adapted to
1970s data, and compares it head-to-head with HAA-Simple and S&P 500 buy & hold.

Generated by `research/stagflation_1970s_bull_stack.py` (reuses the
`stagflation_1970s_tip_canary.py` + `stagflation_1970s_trend_vol.py` harness
verbatim). Artifacts: `stagflation_1970s_bull_stack_findings.json` (+ this file).

## Strategies

- **bull_stack**: risk-on iff ALL three pass -- (a) synthetic-TIP 13612U > 0,
  (b) S&P 500 13612U > 0, (c) rv_60d(S&P daily) < rv_252d(S&P daily). Risk-on ->
  100% S&P 500 TR. Off -> SAFE = best of {cash (3m T-bill), IEF (GS5 par-bond TR)}
  by 13612U.
- **haa_simple**: risk-on iff (synthetic-TIP 13612U > 0) AND (S&P 500 13612U > 0).
  Off -> best of {cash, IEF} by 13612U. (= bull_stack MINUS the vol gate.)
- **buyhold**: always 100% S&P 500 TR.

Monthly rebalance; signal at end of month t applied to month t+1 (1-month lag,
no lookahead). TIP-only canary is used (no HYG/credit risky universe pre-1980).

### Full period 1968-1985

| Strategy | CAGR | MaxDD | Sharpe | Vol |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bull_stack | 10.67% | -12.40% | 0.361 | 7.93% |
| haa_simple | 11.89% | -12.40% | 0.432 | 9.46% |
| buyhold | 8.85% | -39.16% | 0.135 | 12.83% |

Both gated strategies beat buy & hold on CAGR, MaxDD, and Sharpe. The vol gate
costs ~1.2pp CAGR and ~0.07 Sharpe over the full period (haa_simple > bull_stack)
but does not improve full-period MaxDD (both -12.40%, set in 1977-82).

| Strategy | Total ret | MaxDD | DD avoided vs buyhold | Sharpe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bull_stack | +69.98% | -12.40% | +1.02 pp | -0.083 |

concentrated in the sharp 1973-74 crash.

## Comparison to the earlier leg-only 1973-74 results

| Variant | 1973-74 MaxDD |
|---|---|
| trend-only (companion) | -9.22% |
| trend+vol (companion) | -6.85% |
| **bull_stack (this test)** | **-6.85%** |
| haa_simple (canary+trend) | -9.22% |
| buyhold | -39.16% |

The full bull_stack 1973-74 MaxDD (-6.85%) is **identical** to the companion
trend+vol leg result (-6.85%), and haa_simple (-9.22%) is **identical** to
trend-only. This is the direct proof that the canary leg added nothing in 1973-74:
adding the canary on top of trend+vol left MaxDD unchanged, and removing the vol
gate (haa_simple) collapses bull_stack back to the trend-only number. **The vol
gate is what tightens -9.22% down to -6.85%; the canary is inert.**

## Which leg was binding? (canary inertness)

Across all 108 bull_stack defensive months (1968-1985):

| Leg | Months it was failing (binding) | Sole binding leg |
|---|---|---|
| vol | 84 | 41 |
| trend | 67 | 24 |
| canary (TIP) | **1** | **0** |

- The TIP canary failed in exactly **one** month (1980-04), and in that month
  trend AND vol had **already** de-risked. The canary was the **sole** binding
  leg in **0** months -- it never once changed a bull_stack decision.
- Confirmed: the canary leg is present but **non-binding / effectively always-on**
  in the 1970s. All protective work is done by trend (67 months) and vol (84
  months). This matches the companion finding that synthetic-TIP 13612U de-risked
  0 useful months.

### Vol-leg attribution

41 months where bull_stack went defensive but haa_simple stayed risk-on (vol gate
sole extra de-risk): applied S&P mean +1.74%, only 29.3% negative. So the vol
gate's extra de-risking was, on average, into rising months -- it trims tail risk
(critical in 1973-74) at the cost of some upside in choppy-but-rising regimes,
explaining the full-period CAGR drag.

## Whipsaw / defensive-month counts (1968-1985, 216 months)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bull_stack | 108 | 63 | 58.3% | 51.7% | 37 | 71 |

The vol gate roughly doubles time spent defensive (108 vs 67 months) and raises

protection. Safe leg chose cash about 2x more often than IEF (best-of-13612U
favored T-bills through the rising-rate 1970s, as expected).

## Verdict

Through 1970s stagflation, the **actual combined BULL stack would have protected
capital well in the sharp 1973-74 crash** (MaxDD -6.85% vs buy & hold -39.16%, a
**32.3 pp** drawdown avoided; +1.92% total) and would have
trailed slightly through the slow 1977-82 grind (+69.98% vs buy & hold +80.67%,
only +1.02 pp DD avoided). Full period it beats buy & hold on CAGR (10.67% vs
8.85%), MaxDD (-12.40% vs -39.16%), and Sharpe (0.361 vs 0.135).

**Its protection is driven entirely by trend + vol with the canary inert.** The
TIP canary was binding in just 1 of 216 months and never the sole binding leg, so
it never changed a decision; bull_stack's 1973-74 MaxDD is identical to the
trend+vol leg result (-6.85%) and haa_simple's is identical to trend-only
(-9.22%). The vol gate is the marginal improver in the crash (tightening -9.22%
to -6.85%) at the cost of ~1.2pp full-period CAGR from extra whipsaw. In this era
the canary is a present-but-passive safety rail; trend and vol do the work.

## Data / proxy caveats

- **Synthetic TIP**: CPI-accrual proxy (GS5 par-bond TR + realized CPIAUCSL
  inflation); no real TIPS pre-1997. Sign-agreement vs real TIP is 0.84 in the
  post-2000 overlap only -- cannot be validated in the 1970s directly. Its near-
  always-on behavior here is itself a caveat (it cannot have failed to protect if
  it almost never fired).
- **Trend signal** uses Shiller MONTHLY-AVERAGE S&P price (TR-reconstructed);
  averaging smooths/lags turning points vs a true month-end close, possibly
  delaying trend flips.
- **Vol gate** uses Yahoo ^GSPC DAILY close (price-only) -- a DIFFERENT price
  series than the trend signal (documented mismatch between monthly-average and
  daily close).
- **IEF analog** = 5y CMT par-bond duration-only TR (convexity omitted), not the
  real IEF ETF; cash = 3m T-bill.
- **Canary = TIP-only** (no HYG / credit risky universe pre-1980), so this test
  isolates trend+vol with the canary leg present but non-binding by construction
  for this era; it is NOT a HYG-OR-TIP production rerun.
- Warmup months with insufficient history default each gate to pass (no lookahead).
