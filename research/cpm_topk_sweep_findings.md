## Verdict (narrative)

### Q1: Is K=4 a defensible plateau or a fitted spike?

**Defensible plateau / principled convention at the deployment level; a soft local peak standalone.**

- **Blend (what is actually deployed) is a flat plateau across K=4..6.** Clean Sharpe: K4=1.503, K5=1.493, K6=1.490 (spread 0.013). Stress: K4=1.397, K5=1.388, K6=1.380 (spread 0.017). At the 60/20/20 portfolio level K=4 sits at the TOP of a flat plateau, not on a spike. The CPM sleeve is only 60% weight, so the standalone K=4 peak is partly damped by BULL+NDX.
- **Standalone CPM shows K=4 as a genuine local peak with an asymmetric profile**: steep rise on the left (K2=0.737 -> K3=0.973 -> K4=1.263, ~+0.25/step) and a *gentle* decline on the right (K4=1.263 -> K5=1.215 -> K6=1.190, ~-0.04/step). A fitted spike would crater on both sides; here the right shoulder degrades gracefully (soft plateau), which is the signature of a robust optimum, not an overfit point.
- **K=4 is an a-priori rule (ceil(N/2) = top-half of the 8-asset universe), not a grid-search winner.** It lands at/near the optimum on BOTH windows independently and degrades monotonically and gently away from it. Same ranking holds in clean and 27-yr stress (K4 > K5 > K6 > K3 > K2 on standalone Sharpe in both). That cross-window consistency is strong evidence the choice is structural, not curve-fit.
- **Mechanics are coherent**: vol falls monotonically with K (more candidates => more pairs => lower achievable min-variance), while CAGR peaks at K=4 then bleeds. K=4 is the CAGR/vol sweet spot. MaxDD is flat (-15.41% clean) for K=4..6 but K=2,3 are worse (K2 -21.8%), and K=6 worsens in stress (-20.61%). K=4 is also DD-favorable.
- **K=2 (single forced pair = top-2) is clearly inferior everywhere** (clean CPM 0.737, turnover 396%). Widening the pool to enable a pair *search* adds real, monotone value up to K=4. This validates the "pool wide enough to choose a low-variance pair" design.

Bottom line: K=3/4/5 form a top-half plateau; K=4 is the principled center of it, defensible and not fitted. K=5 would be an almost-free alternative (blend Sharpe -0.010) if one wanted a wider, even-more-conservative pool.

### Q2: Does min-var keep momentum integrity or drift into low-vol decoration?

**Momentum integrity is preserved. Min-var is rank-aware, not rank-indifferent.**

- **Selection frequency decays monotonically with worse rank**: rank-1 chosen in 32.6% of pair-members, rank-2 25.1%, rank-3 21.7%, rank-4 20.6%. A pure low-vol grab indifferent to momentum would be flat ~25% each. The downward slope shows min-var systematically favors higher-momentum assets (rank-1 is +12pp over rank-4).
- **Mean selected rank = 2.30** (of 4), and **rank-1 appears in ~65% of pairs** (combos 1+2, 1+3, 1+4 = 26.2+20.3+18.7%). The pair is almost always anchored on the momentum leader.
- **58.8% of months draw the pair entirely from ranks {1,2,3}**; **41.2% include the weakest rank-4 candidate** as a variance-reducing diversifier.
- **The rank-4 grab is NOT low-momentum decoration**: all four candidates have already passed (a) the positive-Faber-trend filter and (b) the vol-adj top-half cut. The "weakest candidate" is the 4th-best of 8 by vol-adj momentum and is positive-trend. So min-var occasionally pairing rank-1/rank-2 with rank-4 is choosing a *qualified* diversifier, not reaching outside the momentum-eligible set.
- **Asset-contribution corroborates this**: top CPM contributor is SPHQ (quality/low-vol factor) at 25.8% clean / 15.7% stress with the highest average weight (28.9% clean). SPHQ is simultaneously high vol-adj-momentum AND low-variance, so the low-vol pick and the high-rank pick coincide -- there is no momentum-vs-variance conflict driving decoration.

### Q3: Asset dependence (QQQ/TLT/GLD)

- **No extreme single-asset dependence.** Clean: SPHQ 25.8%, GLD 20.0%, QQQ 16.2%, TLT 10.6%. US-equity factor (SPHQ+QQQ) = 42% of contribution; diversifiers (GLD+TLT+DBC) = ~38%. Stress window is even more balanced -- no asset exceeds 16% (SPHQ 15.7%, GLD 14.8%, DBC 14.0%, QQQ 12.9%, EFA 10.9%, VNQ 10.7%).
- QQQ specifically contributes 16.2% (clean) / 12.9% (stress) -- meaningful but not dominant. GLD and TLT carry real, expected diversifier weight, consistent with min-var favoring them.

### Stability

- Pair identity vs K=4 baseline: K=3 matches 58.8%, K=5 67.4%, K=6 63.1%; >=1 asset overlap is 95-99.5% for K=3..6. Selection is stable in neighborhood of K=4 -- adjacent K values mostly reshuffle one leg, rarely flip both. K=2 is the outlier (26.2% identical) because forcing a single top-2 pair removes the diversifier choice.

### Recommendation

Keep production at K=4. It is the principled ceil(N/2) top-half convention, sits at the top of a flat blend-level plateau (K=4..6 within 0.013-0.017 Sharpe), is the standalone CAGR/vol sweet spot, reproduces headline numbers exactly, and the min-var pair preserves momentum integrity (rank-aware, mean rank 2.30, anchored on rank-1 ~65% of the time). No evidence of fitting or low-vol drift.
