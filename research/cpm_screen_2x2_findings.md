## 5. Narrative verdict

### Marginal effect of each screen (canary held fixed throughout)

**Positive-Faber (absolute-momentum) filter -- remove via B (K=4, no absmom):**
- CPM standalone Sharpe essentially unchanged: 1.263 -> 1.258 (clean), 1.218 -> 1.177 (stress). CAGR unchanged (14.58% -> 14.54% clean).
- But MaxDD worsens materially: standalone -15.41% -> -17.97% (clean, +2.56pp), blend -9.82% -> -12.05% (+2.23pp). Blend Calmar drops 1.384 -> 1.127.
- Mechanism (section 2): B holds >=1 falling (Faber<=0) asset in only ~5-6% of risk-on months, but those months realize far worse returns than positive-only months (clean 0.59% vs 1.28%; stress 0.01% vs 1.21%). The filter screens out genuinely bad single-asset holdings that the slow HYG/TIP credit canary still lets through.
- Conclusion: the filter is NOT redundant with the canary. It is cheap tail insurance -- ~0 Sharpe cost, ~2-2.5pp of drawdown protection plus the partial-safe de-risking layer (1 risky + 50% safe when <2 positive). Removing it deepens drawdowns for no return benefit.

**Top-K cap -- remove via A (no K, keep absmom):**
- Clearly hurts: CPM standalone Sharpe 1.263 -> 1.171 (clean, -0.092), 1.218 -> 1.120 (stress). CAGR 14.58% -> 12.09% (clean, -2.5pp). Stress MaxDD blows out -15.91% -> -20.61%.
- Mechanism (section 3): unlimited min-var drifts toward low-vol assets regardless of momentum quality. A's mean selected-pair EAA rank drifts 2.30 -> 3.07 and the pair includes a bottom-half (rank>=5) asset in ~43% of months (C0 by construction never does -- K=4 caps candidates to ranks 1-4).
- Conclusion: the K=4 cap is the mechanism that keeps min-var honest -- it confines pair selection to genuine momentum leaders. Removing it lets the optimizer chase variance reduction into weak-trend assets, costing return and (in stress) drawdown.

### The D Sharpe paradox -- do not be fooled

D (no K, no absmom) posts the HIGHEST blend Sharpe (1.410 clean, 1.350 stress). This is a volatility-compression artifact, not genuine edge:
- It achieves high Sharpe by parking in low-vol assets: blend vol 8.00% vs C0 9.84%, turnover collapses to 123% vs 313%, mean pair rank drifts to 4.20 with bottom-half assets in 74.5% of months.
- The compounding mandate is hurt: blend CAGR only 11.56% vs C0 13.59% (-2pp/yr), and 2022 blend return 3.03% vs C0 4.95%.
- Drawdown-adjusted, D is strictly worse: blend Calmar 0.923 vs 1.384 (clean), 0.865 vs 1.072 (stress); blend MaxDD -12.52% vs -9.82%. With no absmom it holds a falling asset 44-47% of months and rides them into deeper drawdowns.
- Net: D buys a higher Sharpe ratio by lowering both numerator and denominator, but loses on every axis the strategy actually cares about (CAGR, MaxDD, Calmar, 2022).

### Recommendation

Production C0 (K=4 + positive-Faber filter) is justified. The two screens are complementary, not redundant:
- **K=4 cap controls rank-quality / return** -- without it (A, D) min-var drifts to low-rank low-vol assets, costing ~2pp CAGR.
- **Positive-Faber filter controls per-asset tail risk** -- without it (B, D) drawdowns deepen ~2-2.5pp and Calmar falls, because the slow credit canary alone does not catch individual asset breakdowns, and the partial-safe de-risking layer is lost.

Simpler alternatives ranked:
- **B (AAA-style, no absmom)** is the closest competitor: near-identical Sharpe (blend 1.357 vs 1.347) and CAGR (13.58% vs 13.59%), and it is genuinely simpler (drop one screen). But it pays ~2.2pp deeper blend drawdown (-12.05% vs -9.82%) and lower Calmar (1.127 vs 1.384). Acceptable only if the extra drawdown is tolerable; not a free win.
- **A (no K)** is dominated by C0 on every axis (lower Sharpe, lower CAGR, worse stress DD). Reject.
- **D (no K + no absmom)** has a deceptively high Sharpe but worse CAGR/Calmar/DD/2022. Reject for a compounding strategy.

Bottom line: keep C0. The positive-Faber filter is low-cost drawdown insurance and the K=4 cap is the rank-quality guardrail; each addresses a distinct failure mode the canary does not cover.

### Caveats
- min-var pair uses a 504d rolling covariance; D/A behavior is sensitive to that lookback (longer lookback would amplify low-vol drift). Not re-swept here.
- Realized per-month returns in section 2 are post-cost holding-period returns attributed to the signal month; cross-window counts differ because the stress window starts 1999.
- absmom filter uses raw Faber score sign (price vs 10mo SMA), matching production; the EAA rank uses the vol-adjusted score. A pair member can be positive-trend yet low-rank.
- Confidence: HIGH on directional contributions (consistent across clean+stress windows and standalone+blend); MEDIUM on exact magnitudes (single execution model, single cov lookback, no bootstrap CI here).
